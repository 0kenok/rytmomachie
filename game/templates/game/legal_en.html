Personal data

You can play without an account. If you create one, the site stores:

  • your username and your password (stored hashed, never in plain text);
  • the games you play, your rating and your ranking results;

This data is only used to run the game: to let you log in, resume your games and appear on the leaderboard. Your username is visible to other players on the leaderboard and in online games. The data is not sold or shared with anyone, and is kept until your account is deleted.
